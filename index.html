mc2sim

Minecraft world → building model → evacuation simulation

1 World

Pick the world's own folder (it contains level.dat) in your Minecraft saves folder: ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/saves on a Mac (press Cmd+Shift+G in the picker), %APPDATA%\.minecraft\saves on Windows. Your browser may call this an upload; the files are only read on this computer.

Loading the simulator…

Your saves are copied first; the originals are never modified.

2 Select the building

Hold Shift and drag on the map (or use Select mode) to draw a box around the building. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan.

3 Building

Floors

Stand at y is the y you stand at on that floor (the Y in Minecraft's F3 screen). Height is how many blocks the floor takes up, from its floor to the floor above; changing it moves every floor above. Then press Apply corrections.

Every floorblocks tall

Corrections

Something misread? Open the Floor plan tab, pick a tool (real exit, not an exit, chair, table…) and click the spot. Corrections are remembered for this area.
